# Finds

- cup.png: sketch of the silver cup
- vault.zip: encrypted archive. Open it with 7-Zip.

The stone gives you the password for vault.zip. For the year, use the
Embassy of Kazakhstan's article "The Golden Warrior of the Kazakh Steppe":
https://www.gov.kz/org/mfa-washington/press/article/details/202222?lang=en

Join the queen's name and the four-digit year. Use lowercase Latin letters,
with no spaces or punctuation. Read dispatch_note.txt after opening the vault.
